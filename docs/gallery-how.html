How it works

From URL to clean spreadsheet in four steps

You stay in the loop before the full run, so there are no surprises at delivery.
1

You send the URL

The listing or category page you want, plus the columns you need. I reply with a short plan and quote.

2

I check the structure

I look at the page layout and confirm which fields exist and how many rows the page yields.

3

You approve a sample

Before the full run you get a small sample extract to confirm the columns are right.

4

I deliver the file

A clean CSV and a Google Sheet link, with one revision included if anything needs adjusting.
